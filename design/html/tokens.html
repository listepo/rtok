Brand constants

  • cyan#5CE1FF
  • coral#FF6B4A
  • navy#06101A
  • ink#0B1A24
  • green#3DDC97
  • amber#F5C451

Cyan is the one accent; coral means Δ (a measured cut) or danger and stays scarce; navy is the dark canvas and the mark tile. Green and amber are status only.

Elevation

  • shadow-e1rows, chips
  • shadow-e2panels, glass
  • shadow-e3toasts, dialogs

Hairline borders before shadows; each level pairs a 1px inner shine line with soft drop shadows. Glass panels stay 82% (dark) / 86% (light) opaque.

Motion & focus

fast 120ms (colour, border) · base 180ms (selection, knobs) · standard cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1) · emphasized cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1). Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every duration collapses to 0.01ms. Focus ring: 2px bg gap + 2px focus stroke, keyboard only.
