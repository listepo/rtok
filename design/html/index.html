rtok — product design

Every screen rtok ships, as static HTML and CSS: the operator admin (web admin and Slint webui share one page model), the public site, the terminal dashboard, plus the tokens, components and assets they are built from. Opens by double-click, no server, no network. Light and dark follow your system until you pick one.

notes

Admin nav labels follow the admin draft (overview); model::pages() titles that page usage in the TUI and Slint tab bar.

graph, hosts, config, services and worktrees have no icon in crates/rtok-webui/ui/icons (Slint falls back to overview.svg); the design shows two-letter monograms there until icons exist.

Six of the admin pages arrive as plain text on the /ws frame; their screens parse that text and keep a raw text toggle with the verbatim string.
